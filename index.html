I am a passionate technologist and scientist with training in bioengineering, computational biology, and molecular biology. Through my roles in academia and the biotech industry, I've led highly interdisciplinary projects within fields such as high-throughput single-cell assay development, epigenetic editing in the context of gene and cell therapy, and antibody discovery and engineering.
