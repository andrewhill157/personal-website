I am currently a computational biologist at Infinimmune. I hold a Ph.D. in Genome Sciences from the University of Washington, where I worked with Jay Shendure and Cole Trapnell. My undergraduate studies were in Bioengineering.

While my current focus is on computational biology, I also have broad experience in molecular biology, genomics, and technology development. I am passionate about projects at the intersection of these fields.
